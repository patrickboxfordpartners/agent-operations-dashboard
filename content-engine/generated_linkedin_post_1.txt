How We Cut Scheduling Time by 68% for a Dental Practice

A dental front desk was drowning in 15 hours of scheduling calls every week. Here's how we fixed it in under 4 months.

The problem was straightforward — patients calling to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments consumed the entire morning. The receptionist couldn't focus on patients actually in the office.

We built a simple AI scheduling assistant using Make and Claude.

→ Patients interact via SMS or web chat
→ AI handles booking, rescheduling & FAQs
→ Complex cases escalate to the front desk automatically
→ Calendar syncs in real time — zero double bookings

The result?

✅ 15 hrs/week → 5 hrs/week
✅ 68% reduction in scheduling time
✅ Full ROI in 3.4 months
✅ Staff now focused on in-person patient experience

No massive software overhaul. No new hires. Just smart automation built on tools that already exist.

If your front desk spends hours on repetitive tasks, there's a good chance AI can give that time back — faster and cheaper than you think.
