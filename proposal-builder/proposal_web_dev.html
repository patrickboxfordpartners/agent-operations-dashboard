## Current State Assessment

Riverside Consulting Group's current website, riversidecg.com, was built in 2015 on WordPress 5.8 with jQuery and Bootstrap 3 — technologies that were reasonable choices a decade ago but are now significantly outdated. A thorough technical and UX audit reveals a site that scores poorly across performance, mobile usability, search visibility, and lead generation effectiveness. The site's 4,200ms average load time places it in the bottom quartile of professional services websites, its mobile score of 42/100 triggers Google's mobile-unfriendly penalty, and a cascade of SEO issues including missing meta descriptions, no structured data, and broken internal links have collectively caused the observed decline in organic search rankings. The site is not equipped to serve as a lead generation asset for a modern consulting practice.

### Strengths
- ✅ Established domain authority: riversidecg.com has years of domain age and likely retains residual backlink equity that a proper SEO rebuild can leverage immediately rather than starting from zero.
- ✅ Existing WordPress foundation: While outdated, the WordPress CMS provides a familiar content management interface for the team, and migration to a modernized WordPress build (rather than a full platform change) reduces retraining time and preserves editorial workflows.
- ✅ Clear service structure: The existing site reflects a reasonably well-organized service taxonomy, giving us a strong content architecture starting point that avoids the ambiguity of a fully undefined site map.
- ✅ Active business with real content assets: With 45 employees and $5M in annual revenue, Riverside has legitimate case studies, team credentials, and thought leadership material that can be surfaced and showcased — the raw material for compelling content exists, it simply isn't being presented effectively.

### Challenges
- ⚠️ Critical mobile usability failure (score: 42/100): Google's mobile-first indexing means your rankings are determined by how well your site performs on mobile devices. A score of 42 is not a minor gap — it represents a site that is actively penalized in search rankings and that delivers a poor experience to the majority of your visitors, who increasingly arrive on phones and tablets.
- ⚠️ Page load time of 4,200ms is driving visitor abandonment: Research consistently shows that conversion rates drop by roughly 4.4% for every additional second of load time. At 4.2 seconds, Riverside is losing a significant percentage of visitors before they ever read a single line of content. This is a direct, quantifiable cost.
- ⚠️ SEO collapse across multiple dimensions simultaneously: Missing meta descriptions on 60% of pages, zero structured data markup, broken internal links disrupting crawl paths, slow server response times, and mobile penalties are not isolated issues — they compound each other. Recovery requires addressing all of them in a coordinated way, not piecemeal fixes.
- ⚠️ Analytics tracking failure means decisions are made blind: If analytics are not tracking properly, Riverside cannot know which services pages attract the most interest, where visitors drop off in the conversion funnel, which traffic sources deliver quality leads, or whether any current marketing investment is working. This is both an immediate operational problem and a historical data loss that limits baseline benchmarking.
- ⚠️ Visual design mismatch undermines brand credibility: A management consulting firm advising organizations on performance, strategy, and operational excellence is implicitly making a claim to competence. A website that looks like 2015 — with dated typography, non-responsive layouts, and static imagery — contradicts that claim at the exact moment a prospective client is evaluating whether to trust you with their most critical business challenges.

### Opportunities
- 💡 Structured data and local SEO can deliver rapid ranking recovery: Implementing schema markup for professional services (Organization, Service, FAQPage, Person schemas) combined with correcting the technical penalties currently suppressing rankings represents a high-ROI, relatively fast improvement. Many consulting firms in this space have sparse technical SEO, meaning a well-executed implementation can deliver meaningful ranking gains within 60–90 days of launch.
- 💡 A conversion-optimized design can turn existing traffic into leads: Even at current depressed traffic levels, a site built around clear calls-to-action, social proof (client outcomes, team credentials), and frictionless contact pathways will convert a materially higher percentage of visitors into consultation requests. Improving conversion rate from a typical 1% to even 2–3% on current traffic volumes represents a significant increase in pipeline without requiring any increase in traffic.
- 💡 Thought leadership content infrastructure can differentiate Riverside in search: Management consulting is a highly competitive search category, but long-form, practitioner-authored content targeting specific problems (e.g., 'operational efficiency for mid-market manufacturers' or 'post-merger integration consulting') can rank well and attract exactly the right buyers. Building a proper blog and resource infrastructure into the new site creates a compounding organic growth asset.

## Proposed Solution

Boxford Partners' approach is built on a core belief: a consulting firm's website should work as hard as its consultants do. That means a site that loads instantly, adapts perfectly to any device, communicates credibility and expertise without friction, and actively converts qualified visitors into consultation requests. We are not proposing a cosmetic refresh of riversidecg.com — we are proposing a ground-up redesign using modern architecture that eliminates every identified technical liability while building the commercial infrastructure Riverside needs to grow.

Our methodology follows a structured four-phase engagement: Discovery and Strategy first establishes the correct foundation — target buyer personas, priority service pages, keyword landscape, and content architecture — so that every design and development decision is made in service of a business outcome, not aesthetic preference. Design and Development translates that strategy into a high-fidelity, mobile-first WordPress build with optimized performance, a proper component library, and a CMS that non-technical staff can confidently maintain. The SEO and Analytics phase ensures the new site launches with a complete technical SEO foundation, proper Google Analytics 4 and Search Console integration, and conversion tracking that gives Riverside actionable data from day one. Finally, a Launch and Optimization phase ensures a smooth go-live, captures early performance data, and makes the iterative improvements that turn a good launch into a strong-performing long-term asset.

### Implementation Phases

#### Phase 1: Phase 1: Discovery, Strategy & Technical Audit
**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks

Before a single pixel is designed, we invest time understanding Riverside Consulting Group's buyers, competitive landscape, and business priorities. This phase combines stakeholder interviews with the leadership team, a deep competitive analysis of 5–8 peer consulting firms, a comprehensive keyword opportunity mapping exercise focused on Riverside's core service areas, and a full technical audit of the current site to document every issue that must be resolved. The output of this phase is a Site Strategy Document that serves as the single source of truth for all subsequent design and development decisions — including sitemap, page hierarchy, target keywords by page, conversion goals, and brand direction.

**Deliverables:**
- Stakeholder interview sessions (up to 4 participants) with synthesized findings report
- Competitive digital analysis of 5 peer consulting firms with benchmark scoring
- Keyword opportunity map identifying 40–60 priority keywords across service areas, with volume and difficulty ratings
- Complete technical SEO audit report documenting all 47+ identified issues with prioritized remediation plan
- Site Strategy Document: approved sitemap, page-level goals, buyer persona profiles, and content requirements matrix

#### Phase 2: Phase 2: UX Design & Brand-Aligned Visual System
**Timeline:** 3–4 weeks
**Dependencies:** Phase 1

With strategy approved, our design team builds the visual and user experience foundation of the new riversidecg.com. This is not a template-based process. We design custom wireframes for all key page types — homepage, service pages, team/about, case study template, contact — followed by high-fidelity mockups that translate Riverside's brand positioning into a visual language that communicates sophistication, trust, and competence. All designs are mobile-first, meaning mobile layouts are designed before desktop, ensuring the experience is excellent on every device. Designs are reviewed and approved by Riverside stakeholders before development begins.

**Deliverables:**
- Wireframes for all primary page templates (minimum 8 page types) with annotated UX rationale
- Mobile-first high-fidelity design mockups in Figma, including desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints
- Interactive prototype for stakeholder review and user testing
- Brand-aligned design system: typography scale, color palette, component library, iconography, and photography direction
- Conversion element specifications: CTA placement strategy, lead capture form design, trust signal integration (logos, testimonials, credentials)

#### Phase 3: Phase 3: Development, Performance Engineering & CMS Build
**Timeline:** 5–7 weeks
**Dependencies:** Phase 1, Phase 2

Approved designs are developed into a production-ready WordPress website engineered for speed, security, and maintainability. We migrate from the current Bootstrap 3 framework to a modern, lightweight custom block theme built on WordPress's native block editor (Gutenberg), eliminating the bloated plugin dependencies and legacy jQuery calls that are contributing to the current 4,200ms load time. Performance is treated as a feature, not an afterthought: we target a sub-1,500ms load time and Google PageSpeed Insights scores of 90+ on mobile and desktop. The CMS is configured so that Riverside's team can update service pages, publish blog posts, and add team members without developer assistance.

**Deliverables:**
- Fully developed, responsive WordPress website built to approved designs across all page templates
- Performance-optimized build targeting <1,500ms load time with Google PageSpeed scores of 90+ (mobile and desktop)
- Content migration of all approved existing pages, team profiles, and service descriptions with SEO-optimized rewrites
- CMS training documentation and 60-minute live training session for content administrators
- Security hardening: SSL, WAF configuration, automated backups, role-based user permissions, and plugin audit

#### Phase 4: Phase 4: SEO Foundation, Analytics & Conversion Tracking
**Timeline:** 2–3 weeks
**Dependencies:** Phase 3

A fast, beautiful website that cannot be found and cannot be measured is a missed opportunity. This phase systematically resolves every SEO issue identified in the Phase 1 audit and builds the analytics infrastructure Riverside needs to make data-driven decisions going forward. Every page receives an optimized meta title and description. Structured data markup (Organization, Service, FAQPage, and Person schemas) is implemented site-wide. The broken internal link structure is repaired and a logical internal linking strategy is implemented to distribute page authority efficiently. Google Analytics 4 is configured with custom events tracking consultation form submissions, PDF downloads, and key engagement milestones. A Google Search Console integration and initial sitemap submission initiates the re-crawl process with Google.

**Deliverables:**
- Complete on-page SEO implementation: meta titles and descriptions for all pages, header tag optimization, alt text audit, and keyword-to-page alignment per the strategy map
- Structured data markup implementation: Organization, Service, FAQPage, and Person schemas validated via Google's Rich Results Test
- Repaired internal link architecture with crawl path validation via Screaming Frog post-launch audit
- Google Analytics 4 configuration with custom conversion events: form submissions, phone clicks, content downloads, and scroll depth milestones
- Google Search Console setup, XML sitemap submission, and initial crawl monitoring report delivered 30 days post-launch

#### Phase 5: Phase 5: Launch, QA & 90-Day Post-Launch Optimization
**Timeline:** 2 weeks launch + 90-day monitoring
**Dependencies:** Phase 4

Go-live is managed as a carefully orchestrated process, not a single switch-flip. Pre-launch QA covers cross-browser and cross-device testing, 301 redirect mapping from all existing URLs to preserve any accumulated link equity, form and tracking verification, and a final performance benchmark. Following launch, Boxford Partners remains actively engaged for 90 days: monitoring Google Search Console for crawl errors, reviewing Analytics data weekly, and delivering two rounds of conversion rate optimization (CRO) improvements based on real user behavior data. This post-launch partnership is what transforms a successful launch into a sustained performance improvement.

**Deliverables:**
- Pre-launch QA report: cross-browser testing (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge), cross-device testing (iOS/Android), and broken link scan
- 301 redirect map implementation for all existing URLs to preserve link equity and prevent 404 errors
- Launch-day monitoring and immediate issue resolution (dedicated support during go-live window)
- Two rounds of post-launch CRO iterations based on Analytics and heatmap data (delivered at 45 and 90 days)
- 90-day performance report: traffic trends, ranking movement for target keywords, conversion rate, and lead volume benchmark
